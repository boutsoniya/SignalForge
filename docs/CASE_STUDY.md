# SignalForge Case Study

## Context
I am an AI/ML student targeting product management internships. Instead of presenting another ML model, I built SignalForge to demonstrate how technical fluency can support product decisions.

## Discovery framing
The product starts from a common workflow problem: customer feedback exists, but evidence, prioritization, and outcomes are disconnected.

## Product decisions
**Decision 1 — transparency over black-box AI.** Every score exposes its inputs.

**Decision 2 — prioritize problems, not requests.** A feature request is evidence; the underlying user problem is the roadmap unit.

**Decision 3 — metrics before shipping.** Every major opportunity gets a hypothesis and success metric.

## Trade-off
The prototype deliberately avoids production integrations in v1. This keeps the MVP focused on the highest-risk assumption: whether structured evidence actually improves prioritization.

## What I would do next
1. Interview 5–8 target users.
2. Test the manual-vs-SignalForge workflow.
3. Measure decision time and confidence.
4. Reweight the scoring model from observed behavior.
5. Only then invest in LLM and project-management integrations.

## PM takeaway
The goal is not to prove that AI can summarize feedback. The goal is to prove that **better synthesis changes a product decision**.
