# Roadmap

## Now — Prove the decision loop
- Feedback inbox
- Transparent opportunity scoring
- RICE ranking
- Now/Next/Later roadmap
- Decision log

**Exit criterion:** a new feedback item can be traced to a ranked opportunity and roadmap decision.

## Next — Validate with users
- 5–8 PM/student interviews
- Usability test on prioritization workflow
- Compare manual vs SignalForge decision time
- Tune scoring weights from observed behavior

**Exit criterion:** median time-to-decision improves without reducing reviewer confidence.

## Later — Connect the workflow
- CSV/API ingestion
- Jira/Linear integration
- LLM-assisted summarization with citations
- Team workspaces and permissions
- Historical outcome learning

## Explicit trade-offs
We are prioritizing **decision quality and explainability over automation** in v1. A flashy AI feature that cannot show evidence would weaken the core product thesis.
