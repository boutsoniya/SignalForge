MVP

Help users understand how close they are to value.

Problem: new users abandon onboarding when they cannot tell what remains.

Evidence: 41 signals across Support, Reviews and Interviews; 19% of active users encounter the friction.

Hypothesis: a visible progress indicator will increase onboarding completion because users can understand remaining effort and resume state.

Scope: progress component, resume state, completion event.

Non-goals: redesigning the entire onboarding flow.

Acceptance: progress persists across sessions; completion emits an analytics event; keyboard navigation remains intact.
